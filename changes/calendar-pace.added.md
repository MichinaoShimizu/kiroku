- "A day to match last week" (or last month) in the key figures of a week or month in progress: the previous period's total minus the total so far, divided by the days left, for estimated cost and Kiro credits side by side. Each day still to come in the week calendar shows the same amount as "To match" in a dashed box (the month calendar shows it as plain text, explained under the calendar), and its breakdown puts the commits of both periods side by side. Once the period passes the previous total, the key figures say by how much. It needs no budget setting
