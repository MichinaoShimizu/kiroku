What you see, and what it comes from

On the imageFrom the history
ColumnA date. Starts at the first day with history (4 weeks if there is less); with less than 8 weeks of history it ends at the last one
HeightThe time of day, 6:00 at the top to 6:00 the next morning at the bottom, so late-night work lands under the previous day
A streakOne stretch of active time in a session, estimated from the history's timestamps (it includes time the AI ran on its own). A 15-minute gap starts a new streak (--gap)
LengthHow long that stretch was active
ColorThe agent, or the project when one agent has 90% of your time (names never on the image)
Claude CodeCodexKiro IDEKiro CLIKiro Crew
BrightnessLight adds up where streaks overlap, so sessions run in parallel glow brighter
Dark bandsDays and hours when no session was active
Amber labelsWhat stands out, above the streaks: the busiest month (30%+ above the rest), the first day of an agent you started later, and, only if you tick it, the longest stretch without AI (7+ days, no dates)
TotalsHours with a session active (parallel sessions count once; includes time the AI ran on its own), sessions, commits and pull requests. Small, and can be left off

Never on the image: prompts, project names, branches, files or cost. Late nights, weekends and the longest stretch without AI only if you tick them. It is drawn in your browser and sent nowhere.
